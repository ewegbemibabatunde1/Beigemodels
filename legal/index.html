Pre-Launch Governance Statement: This page establishes Beige Model Management’s operating standards, talent safety commitments, and privacy principles ahead of official commercial release.

01. On-Set Welfare & Working Conditions

All production clients booking talent through Beige agree to provide safe, professional, and respectful working environments. This includes mandatory rest breaks, proper nourishment, private and secure dressing areas, reasonable call times, and chaperone accommodations for young or emerging talent.

02. Zero Tolerance for Exploitation or Harassment

Beige Model Management strictly prohibits any form of discrimination, harassment, intimidation, or inappropriate conduct on set, during fittings, or in agency communications. Any client, photographer, or contractor violating these rules will be immediately blacklisted and reported to relevant authorities.

03. Transparent Representation & Contracts

We operate on clear, written model management and agency agreements. Models receive complete transparency regarding booking rates, agency commissions, tax deductions, and client payment schedules. No hidden deductions or arbitrary administrative fees are ever levied against represented models.

04. Privacy & Image Rights

Photographs and likenesses published on this platform are displayed exclusively with model consent and verified agency agreements. We do not sell or lease personal talent contact details to any third parties. Inquiries submitted via WhatsApp or email are handled strictly by authorized agency staff.
