This document establishes the creative direction and design benchmarks for the Beige Models digital transformation, synthesizing principles from six internationally acclaimed digital brands into an original luxury identity for Beige Models.

## 2. The Beige Models Synthesis

The result is not a collage or imitation, but an original, unmistakable digital identity for Beige Models:

* **Color Discipline:** 
  - Primary Pink (`#E71469`) acts as the singular focal point for conversions, key badges, and interactive moments.
  - Secondary Pink (`#F64D9C`) and Deep Pink (`#B2073E`) provide sophisticated depth and hover states.
  - Warm White (`#FCF5F7`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), and Blush (`#F7DCE5`) create an airy, editorial canvas.
  - Obsidian Black (`#0B0808`) and Rich Espresso (`#635252`) anchor headlines and readable narrative copy.
* **Typography Harmony:**
  - `Playfair Display` provides timeless, high-fashion editorial gravitas.
  - `Manrope` provides modern geometric precision, readability, and clean UI clarity.
* **Talent-First Architecture:**
  - Models are presented with dignity, agency verification, comp-card measurements, and verified campaign credits.
  - Clear dual conversion paths: one for commercial brands/casting directors, one for aspiring models.
