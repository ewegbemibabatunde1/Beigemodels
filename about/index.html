Beige Model Management is built around distinctive talent, thoughtful representation and meaningful creative partnerships. The agency's stated mission is to discover authentic potential, nurture sustainable professional careers and curate inspiring partnerships with brands and creative teams.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria, Beige presents women talent for editorial, runway, beauty and commercial opportunities across Africa and international markets.
