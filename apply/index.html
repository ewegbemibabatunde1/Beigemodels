Application Standards

What We Look For
& Digital Guidelines.

We look for authentic individuality, discipline, natural beauty, and strong presence. We represent women across commercial, beauty, editorial, and fashion divisions.

01. Natural Digitals (Polaroids)

Please provide 4 clear, well-lit photos taken in natural daylight on your phone:
• Front headshot (neutral expression)
• Side profile (left or right)
• Waist-up (relaxed smile)
• Full-length front view

02. Styling & Appearance

• Zero makeup (completely bare, clean skin)
• Hair pulled back away from your face
• Plain fitted tank top or t-shirt
• Fitted black jeans or leggings
• Barefoot or plain flat shoes
• No filters, editing, or retouched photos

03. Key Information

• Height (cm) & accurate measurements (bust, waist, hips)
• Current location / city of residence
• Age (applicants under 18 must have parental/guardian consent)
• Instagram / TikTok handle (if active)

Scouting Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior professional experience or an expensive portfolio?

No. Beige actively discovers and develops new faces. High-fashion and commercial agencies prefer clean, un-retouched smartphone digitals (polaroids) over stylized studio shoots. We assess raw bone structure, facial symmetry, skin tone, natural height, and confidence.

Are there any application or registration fees?

Absolutely zero. Beige Model Management will NEVER ask you for money to audition, register, or apply. Any individual claiming to represent Beige who asks for an upfront fee or gift card is an impostor and should be reported immediately.

What are the general height guidelines?

For high-fashion runway and international catwalk editorial, preferred height is 5'9" (175cm) to 6'0" (183cm). For commercial, beauty, cosmetics, and hair divisions, we represent talent from 5'7" (170cm) upwards with exceptional facial features, skin, and presence.

How quickly will I receive feedback after applying?

Our scouting board reviews incoming applications on a weekly cycle. If your profile matches upcoming client briefs or board requirements, our talent liaison will contact you within 5 to 7 business days via WhatsApp or email to schedule an in-person or video call-back.

Can I apply if I live outside Lagos or internationally?

Yes. While our headquarters are in Lagos, Nigeria, Beige represents talent across Africa (Abuja, Port Harcourt, Accra, Nairobi, Johannesburg) and arranges direct international representation for overseas placements.
