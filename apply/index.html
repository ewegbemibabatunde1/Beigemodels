Model Safety & Anti-Scam Advisory

Beige Model Management NEVER charges application fees, registration costs, or portfolio fees. We never request nude, lingerie, or revealing photos. All official scouting inquiries originate exclusively from our verified WhatsApp line (+234 907 717 8197) and official email addresses ending in @beigemodel.org. If you are approached by someone claiming to represent Beige asking for money or inappropriate photos, do not engage and report them immediately.

Application Standards

What We Look For
& Digital Guidelines.

We look for authentic individuality, discipline, natural beauty, and strong presence. We represent women across commercial, beauty, editorial, and fashion divisions.

01. Natural Digitals (Polaroids)

Please provide 4 clear, well-lit photos taken in natural daylight on your phone:
• Front headshot (neutral expression)
• Side profile (left or right)
• Waist-up (relaxed smile)
• Full-length front view

02. Styling & Appearance

• Zero makeup (completely bare, clean skin)
• Hair pulled back away from your face
• Plain fitted tank top or t-shirt
• Fitted black jeans or leggings
• Barefoot or plain flat shoes
• No filters, editing, or retouched photos

03. Key Information

• Height (cm) & accurate measurements (bust, waist, hips)
• Current location / city of residence
• Age (applicants under 18 must have parental/guardian consent)
• Instagram / TikTok handle (if active)
